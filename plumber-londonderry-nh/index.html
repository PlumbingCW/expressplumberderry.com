Before We Arrive in Londonderry

These steps limit damage while a plumber is on the way. They need nothing more than a flashlight.

  • Close the main water shutoff by turning it fully clockwise.
  • Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain the lines.
  • Switch off the water heater if the tank or lines are leaking.
  • Keep people away from wet outlets and the electrical panel.
  • Photograph the damage for your insurer, then call.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility before you call a plumber.

Londonderry notes for plumbing decisions

Woodmont Commons, a 600-acre planned unit development, and other newer neighborhoods mean many Londonderry houses have modern PEX or copper supply lines, while homes near the older village centers can have older piping. Ask what your house has before you assume. Knowing the age of your plumbing helps you judge whether a repair or a repiping quote makes sense.

Number Eight Hill rises to 535 feet, and homes on slopes shed water toward lower neighbors. A sump pump with a clear discharge matters most for houses at the bottom of a grade. The sump pump page covers pits, pumps and battery backups.

Some Londonderry streets use town sewer and water, and others rely on private wells and septic systems. Confirm with the town which service your street has, because a septic problem and a sewer problem look alike but need different fixes.
