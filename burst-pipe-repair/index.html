Close the main water shutoff by turning it fully clockwise. It is usually where the line enters the cellar or utility room. Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain the lines, and switch off the water heater so it does not heat an empty tank. Then call the 24/7 line.

Why pipes burst in New Hampshire

Water expands when it freezes, and the ice plug in a pipe pushes pressure on both sides. The split often forms where the ice formed, but water only appears after the ice melts. A pipe that froze last night can look fine until the house warms up this afternoon.

Pipes in unheated spaces face the highest risk. Cellars with exposed lines, crawlspaces, exterior walls, garages and outdoor spigots are the usual places. A pipe can also fail from corrosion, a loose joint or high water pressure, and those failures happen in July as often as January.

If a pipe is frozen but not yet split

A frozen pipe is a warning. These steps lower the chance of a split while you wait for the plumber.

  1. Open the faucet. Open the tap on the frozen line so water can flow as the ice melts.
  2. Warm the pipe gently. Use a hair dryer or warm towels, working from the faucet toward the frozen section.
  3. Never use an open flame. A torch can ignite framing and damage fittings.
  4. Call if you cannot find or reach the frozen section. A plumber can locate and thaw it safely.

After the water stops

Stopping the water is the first job and drying the structure is the second. Wet drywall, insulation and flooring can grow mold within a couple of days, so arrange drying promptly. Photograph everything, keep damaged items until the insurer sees them and save receipts for the emergency work.

The plumber repairs the pipe and checks nearby lines for damage. If the cause was a frozen section, the plumber will suggest insulation or rerouting. Where a house has repeated freezes, the answer may be a permanent change, such as moving a line out of an exterior wall.

Derry houses and freeze risk

Older farmhouses and capes around Derry Village and East Derry often have supply lines in exterior walls and unheated additions. Newer homes in Derry, Londonderry and Windham can still have lines under cold garage floors or in cantilevered rooms. Rockingham County has had winter storm and ice storm declarations in past decades, so extended outages are possible, and a power loss can leave pipes unprotected.

If you leave the house in winter, keep the heat on, open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls and ask a neighbor to check the house during extended cold spells. If a slow leak appears after the thaw, our leak detection page explains how the plumber locates it.
