About Derry Express Plumbing

An emergency plumbing line for Derry, NH that answers the phone, explains the next step and sends a plumber.

Call (603) 815-9179

Who we are

Derry Express Plumbing is an emergency plumbing line for Derry, New Hampshire. We take calls 24 hours a day and send plumbers to homes and businesses in Derry, Londonderry, Windham, Hampstead, Chester and Salem.

We built this site to be useful before you call. Each service page explains symptoms, repair options and cost factors, and each town page lists the roads, landmarks and local notes that matter for plumbing there.

How a job works

You call the 24/7 line and describe the problem. The dispatcher gives you safe next steps, such as which valve to close, and arranges a plumber. On site, the plumber diagnoses the cause, explains the options and gives you a clear price before work starts.

If the diagnosis shows something outside plumbing, such as a failed well pump or a full septic tank, the plumber says so and points you toward the right trade. See the burst pipe repair and drain cleaning pages for examples of what the first visit covers.

What you can verify yourself

We would rather you check than take our word for it. These are the things worth confirming with any plumber who comes to your house.

  • Ask to see the plumber's New Hampshire license and confirm it with the state's Office of Professional Licensure and Certification.
  • Ask for the price in writing or by text before work starts.
  • Ask whether a permit applies, and confirm with the Derry code enforcement office at 603-432-6148.
  • Keep the old part if you want proof the repair was needed.
  • Call us back if anything about the work is unclear after the plumber leaves.

What we do not claim

We do not publish review counts, awards or company-age claims on this site, because we would rather show you how we work than ask you to trust a number. When we have verifiable credentials or customer feedback to share, they will appear here with their source.

What the line does and does not do

Derry Express Plumbing is a dispatch operation. We answer the phone, learn what is happening and send a plumber. We do not run a showroom, and we do not sell anything on this website. The point of the site is to help you decide what to do before you call and to explain what happens when you do.

Each page describes a problem in plain terms. You will find the symptoms, the usual causes, the repair options and the questions worth asking a plumber. Where the town publishes useful facts, such as the water department's lead line inventory, we link to the source and say what it covers and what it does not.

How we write the pages on this site

The service and town pages describe how plumbing problems work and what a visit involves. They do not promise outcomes, and they do not quote prices, because a price depends on the pipe, the access and the time of the call. When a page mentions a local fact, such as a road, a landmark or an official phone number, it comes from a public source that we checked.

We avoid claims we cannot back. We do not post review counts, star ratings or awards. We do not say how long we have been in business. If you want to judge a plumber, ask to see their license and ask for the price in writing. Those two steps tell you more than any badge on a website.

Where we work

We cover Derry (ZIP 03038) and five nearby towns: Londonderry (03053), Windham (03087), Hampstead (03841), Chester (03036) and Salem (03079). Each town has its own page with roads, landmarks and plumbing notes. The service area page lists them with short summaries.

Towns just outside that list, such as Hudson, Raymond and Atkinson, are handled case by case. The dispatcher knows where plumbers are working at that hour and tells you honestly whether we can reach you. We would rather say no than promise a visit that traffic or weather will break.

Reaching a person

Call (603) 815-9179 for anything urgent. Email works for non-urgent questions. If you have a complaint or a question about work that was done, say so on the call or in an email, and we will follow up. Our plumbing services page lists every topic we cover, from drain cleaning to boiler repair. For anything urgent, call (603) 815-9179 any hour.

Why we say what we cannot do

A plumbing dispatcher who promises everything cannot deliver it. Saying no to a job outside our trades or outside our range is part of being useful. If you need a well driller, a septic pumper or a heating contractor, we will say so.
