Call the 24/7 line

The fastest way to reach us is the phone. Call (603) 815-9179 any time, including nights, weekends and holidays. The dispatcher asks what is happening, where you are and whether water is still running, then tells you the next safe step.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Where we work

Derry Express Plumbing takes calls for Derry, Londonderry, Windham, Hampstead, Chester and Salem, New Hampshire. There is no walk-in counter, so please call instead of visiting. The map below shows the area we cover.

Email contact@expressplumberderry.com for non-urgent questions. For anything involving water that is moving, call instead of writing.

What to have ready when you call

A few details help the dispatcher assign the right plumber and parts.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether you have shut off the main water valve.
  • Whether the house is on town water or a private well.
  • The make and age of the water heater, if the problem is hot water.
