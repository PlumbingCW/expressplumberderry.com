Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit this website or call our line.

Call (603) 815-9179

What we collect

This website does not use forms, accounts or comment boxes. We do not ask you to type personal details into the site. When you call, we collect the information you give us on the call, such as your name, address and the plumbing problem, so we can send a plumber.

Like most sites, our hosting provider keeps basic server logs, such as the pages requested and the time. We use them to keep the site working.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start and ask for another way to share the details.

How we use information

We use call details to dispatch a plumber, give you a price and follow up on the job. We do not sell your information. We share job details with the plumber assigned to your address so the work can be done.

Third-party content

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google map. Those services receive your browser's normal request data when the page loads. Their own privacy policies explain how they handle it.

Questions

Email contact@expressplumberderry.com or call (603) 815-9179 with questions about this policy. We may update it, and the date of the latest update is 2026-11-12.
