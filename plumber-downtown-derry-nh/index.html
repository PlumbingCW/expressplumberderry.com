Downtown Derry at a glance

Downtown Derry sits near the western edge of town, where NH 102 and NH 28 cross. West Broadway is the main street. Manning Street, Martin Street, South Avenue and Abbott Court appear in the town's historical walking tour. A former train depot now anchors the main commercial district, and the old rail line is now the Rockingham Recreational Trail.

Because the area mixes homes, apartments and businesses, calls here can come from very different buildings. A kitchen sink in a single-family house and a floor drain in a storefront need different tools, so the first question is always what kind of building it is.

A short history that explains the buildings

Downtown Derry grew around industry and the railroad. These dates come from the Derry Public Library's historical walking tour.

1846Harvey Perley Hood arrives in Derry and starts a milk cart business
1887 and 1889Coburn Fuller and Co. makes shoes at West Broadway and South Avenue, and the Pillsbury Shoe Factory follows
1882 to 1883A major fire leads the town to create a fire district in March 1883
1903Adams Memorial Hall is built, and the building now houses the Derry Opera House

Mixed-use buildings and what that means for plumbing

Older downtown blocks often put businesses on the ground floor and apartments above. The walking tour records examples, such as the Pillsbury Apartments on West Broadway and lodging rooms on the top floor of the Derry National Bank building. In a building like that, a clog upstairs can back up into a unit or a shop below, so the shared line matters more than the single fixture.

We cannot say how any current building is plumbed. What we can say is how to approach it. Find out who owns the shared drain, which floor each fixture drains to and where the cleanout is. Then a drain cleaning call or a camera inspection starts from facts instead of guesses.

If water is moving in a shared building

Act in this order, then call.

  • Tell the people on the floors below.
  • Close the fixture shutoff or the unit shutoff if you know where it is.
  • Ask the owner or manager where the building's main shutoff is.
  • Keep people and electronics away from the wet area.
  • Photograph the damage before cleanup.

Call before you move furniture so the plumber can see where the water started.
