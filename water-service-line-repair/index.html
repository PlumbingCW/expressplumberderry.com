When the service line fails

A broken service line can flood a yard or cellar, or it can fail quietly under the lawn. Wet ground with no rain, a sudden drop in water pressure and a water meter that spins with everything off all point to the line. Close the main shutoff inside the house and call the 24/7 line.

If water is coming up outside, note where and tell the dispatcher. The plumber can often shut it off at the curb stop or arrange for the utility to do it. Do not dig into a wet area yourself, because other utilities may run nearby.

  • Water bubbling up in the yard with no rain.
  • A sudden loss of water pressure at every tap.
  • A meter that runs when all fixtures are off.
  • A wet cellar wall where the line enters the house.
  • No water at all and a main shutoff that is already on.

What a service line is

The water service line runs from the main in the street to the meter or the main shutoff in your house. In many towns the utility owns the portion up to the meter or curb stop, and the homeowner owns the rest. Ownership rules differ, so ask the water department which section is yours before you hire anyone.

In Derry, the water department serves about 15,000 residents, institutions and businesses and can be reached at 603-432-6147. Homes on private wells have no service line from the street, but they have a line from the well to the house, and many of the same failure patterns apply.

Lead and galvanized service lines in Derry

The Derry Water Department published a 2025 lead service line inventory covering 4,466 lines. It lists 0 as lead, 2,302 as non-lead, 20 as galvanized requiring replacement and 2,144 as unknown. Owners of lines classified as galvanized requiring replacement or unknown can request a free lead test from the town.

If you do not know what your service line is made of, you can identify it where it enters your cellar. A magnet and a scratch test show whether it is steel or soft metal. Ask the plumber to check, and contact the water department about the free test. Source: derrynh.gov water department, lead service line inventory 2025.

Digging, permits and utilities

Before anyone digs, call 811 so utility lines are marked. A service line replacement may need a permit or inspection, and the Derry code enforcement office at 603-432-6148 can confirm what applies. The water department may require its own approval for work at the main connection.

The plumber coordinates these steps and gives you a clear price before digging begins. Where the lawn or driveway will be disturbed, the plumber explains how it will be restored.

Low pressure is not always the service line

Low pressure can come from a failing pressure regulator, a partly closed valve, a clogged fixture or a leak in the house, so the plumber tests before digging. The plumber checks pressure at the hose bib, tests the regulator and uses the meter to look for flow. Our leak detection page explains the meter test.
