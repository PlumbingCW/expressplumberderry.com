East Derry and the Upper Village

East Derry is an unincorporated community inside the town of Derry. It sits on a hill about 2 miles east of downtown Derry, close to the geographic center of town, at an elevation of about 430 feet. Its ZIP code is 03041. Scots-Irish Presbyterians settled it in 1719, which makes it Derry's oldest settlement.

The Upper Village belonged to Londonderry from 1722, and Derry became its own town in 1827. That history matters for one practical reason. The village holds some of the oldest buildings in town, so the age of a house is often the first thing to find out before you choose a repair.

Older houses: what that means for plumbing, and what it does not

A house built in the early 1800s may have been updated many times. Age alone does not tell you what pipes are inside the walls, so we do not assume galvanized supply lines or old drain materials. The reliable way to know is to look, and a camera on the sewer line or a visit from the plumber shows what is really there.

What age does change is the checklist. Older houses often have additions, partial updates and shutoff valves that nobody has labeled. Before any repair, find the main water shutoff and learn which fixtures each valve controls, because that knowledge shortens every emergency.

Before you call about an East Derry house

A few details help the plumber plan the visit and bring the right parts.

  • Find the main water shutoff and check that it turns.
  • Note roughly when the house was built and what has been replaced.
  • Photograph the water heater label and any visible pipe.
  • Check whether the house is on town sewer or a septic tank, and whether it uses town water or a private well.
  • Write down when the problem started and what changed that week.

If water cannot be stopped, close the main valve and call. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 first.

Winter and exterior walls

Hilltop houses in New Hampshire face wind and hard freezes, and pipes in exterior walls and unheated additions are the first to freeze. That is general New England guidance, not a finding about East Derry streets. If a faucet runs slowly in a cold snap, open it a little, warm the wall gently and see the burst pipe repair page for what to do if a pipe splits.
