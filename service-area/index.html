How we chose these towns

Each town on this page borders Derry or sits on a main road leading out of it. Londonderry is next door along NH 102 and NH 28. Windham and Hampstead sit on NH 111, and Chester is reached on NH 102 and NH 121. Salem is the farthest at roughly 12 miles by road, and we show drive times as estimates, because traffic changes them.

Towns that sit just beyond this list, such as Hudson, Raymond, Atkinson and Auburn, are served case by case. Ask the dispatcher, who can tell you whether a plumber can reach you at that hour.

What changes from town to town

Plumbing needs shift with the infrastructure. Parts of Hampstead and Chester depend on private wells and septic systems, while many Derry and Salem streets connect to town water and sewer. Confirm with your town which service your street has, because it changes which problems are likely and who is responsible for the pipe.

The state health department recommends that private well owners test their water regularly. Our leak detection and sewer inspection pages explain what we can check inside the house.

What each town page contains

Every town page follows the same purpose and a different shape. It gives the ZIP code, the main roads, a few landmarks and the plumbing notes that matter in that town, such as wells and septic systems in Hampstead and Chester or large retail and older neighborhoods in Salem. We write each one from public town and state sources.

You will also find a map, a short list of what to do before the plumber arrives and an FAQ about coverage. If you live near a town line, check both pages, because the notes differ. A house on the Londonderry side of the border may have town water, while a house a mile away is on a well.

How long a visit takes to arrange

Drive times depend on where the nearest plumber is, not on a fixed route. A plumber finishing a job in Windham may reach Londonderry faster than one leaving Derry. The dispatcher asks for your address and gives you an honest estimate. We do not guarantee arrival windows, since traffic on NH 28 or I-93 and weather can change them.

If you are at the edge of our coverage, such as Salem, call early and describe the problem fully. A clear description lets the dispatcher decide what parts to load and which plumber to send, and it often shortens the visit. You can reach the line at (603) 815-9179 at any hour.

Wells, septic and town services across the area

The six towns differ in how water and waste reach the house. Derry runs a town water department that serves about 15,000 residents, institutions and businesses. Many homes in Hampstead, Chester, Windham and Londonderry use private wells and septic systems instead of town water and sewer. Confirm with your town which setup your street has.

That choice matters when something fails. A pipe leak on a well system can run the pump continuously, and a drain problem on a septic system may be a full tank. We repair the plumbing inside the house, and we tell you when a job belongs to a well contractor or a septic contractor.

When your town is not on the list

Hudson, Raymond, Atkinson and Auburn sit beside the towns we cover. We do not have pages for them, and we do not claim to serve them all the time. Call the line, give the address and the dispatcher tells you straight away whether a plumber can reach you. If we cannot, we will say so and suggest you call another plumber nearby.

Neighborhoods inside Derry

Some calls come from a part of town rather than a whole town. We wrote a page for each of three areas where public sources give us something real to say: East Derry, downtown Derry and Beaver Lake.

Each page lists what we could confirm and what we could not. None of them claims exact boundaries, because the sources do not give any.
